# Scrum Study Studio: source-to-content register

Content version 1.2.0. Source audit completed 2026-10-14. 232 dated learning-item reviews.

## Authority and verification

Current English Scrum Guide governs Scrum rules. Official Scrum.org articles support named supplemental concepts and situational practices. May 2024 EBM Guide is a separate framework. Reading lists and collections guide study, not precise proof of every claim.

The per-item record is [source-audit.json](source-audit.json). It ties review status and reading evidence to a digest of each item, so subsequent edits cannot silently inherit a completed review. The exact competency mapping is [competency-map.json](competency-map.json). Automatic checks validate those records; they do not independently judge content accuracy.

Counts: 16 modules, 160 original questions (80 shared / 40 PSM / 40 PSPO), eight scenarios / 17 decisions, 48 flashcards.
